
# Hi, I'm Sarina 👋

I'm a **Master of Mathematics (MMath) student in Computer Science** at the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/computer-science/), where I'm supervised by [Prof. Florian Kerschbaum](https://crysp.uwaterloo.ca/) and am a member of the **Cryptography, Security, and Privacy (CrySP) research group**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **trustworthy machine learning, AI security, and generative models**. I'm particularly interested in understanding how machine learning systems work, why they fail, and how we can make them more reliable and secure.

Previously, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science at **Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic)**.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- **Trustworthy AI:** Model security, robustness, watermarking, and reliability
- **Generative Models:** Diffusion models and representation learning
- **Time-Series Learning:** Anomaly detection and temporal representations
- **AI for Healthcare:** Foundation models for EEG analysis and neurological disease detection

### 📚 Selected Research

**[FourStat: Time-Series Anomaly Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13518)**  
A dual-autoencoder framework combining frequency-domain and stationarity-based representations for time-series anomaly detection.

**NeuroWave: EEG-Based Parkinson's Disease Classification**  
Investigating how wavelet-based features and pretrained foundation models can improve Parkinson's disease classification using EEG signals.

**AST-Based Watermarking for Code Generation**  
Exploring structural watermarking techniques for generated code, with a focus on detectability, robustness to code transformations, and functional correctness.

**Tangent-Guided Diffusion**  
Investigating guidance techniques for diffusion models using tangent-space projections.
